# TTM 0.1.0

* Initial CRAN submission.
* `TTM()` and `TTM_linear()` fit weighted GEE terminal trend models on the
  retrospective time scale; `RetroJM()` fits the two-stage retrospective joint
  model with multiply imputed death times.
* `get_beta_mu()`, `get_beta_A()`, `plot_beta_mu()`, `plot_beta_A()`, and
  `test_beta_slope()` summarize the fitted trajectories.
